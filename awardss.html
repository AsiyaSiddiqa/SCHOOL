★ Official Govt. Appreciation

Official Letter of Appreciation 2026

This official letter of appreciation was issued by the Office of the District Education Officer (SE), Mandi Bahauddin, acknowledging the outstanding performance and services of GGHSS Mianwal Ranjha in the SSC (Matriculation) Results 2026. The institution achieved a remarkable pass percentage of 84.29% with 118 students passing out of 140.

In recognition of the exemplary leadership, hard work, and dedication toward quality education, the District Education Officer gracefully appreciated the principal and teaching staff, recommending special recognition for their extraordinary efforts in advancing free and compulsory education.

Issued By: District Education Officer (SE), Mandi Bahauddin

Date: August 8, 2026

★ Distinction Honor

Official Letter of Acknowledgement 2025

This official letter of acknowledgement was issued by the District Education Authority, Mandi Bahauddin, honoring Ms. Farwa Nawaz and GGHSS Mianwal Ranjha for securing an outstanding pass percentage of 91.80% in the BISE Exams (SSC-1st Annual, 2025).

The authority formally recognized her diligence, leadership, and dedication to duty, commending her exceptional contribution to public education and school progress as a great service to the nation.

Issued By: District Education Officer (SE) & Chief Executive Officer (DEA), Mandi Bahauddin

Date: August 12, 2025
